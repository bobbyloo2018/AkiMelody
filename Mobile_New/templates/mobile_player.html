SPOTIFY

Import a playlist

Bring a public Spotify playlist into AkiMelody.

Open a Spotify playlist, tap Share, copy its link, and paste it below.

Follow these steps to share your Spotify Liked Songs:

  1. Open & Login to the Spotify App on your computer.
  2. Click on your 'Liked Songs' Playlist.
  3. Press 'Ctrl' + 'A' on your keyboard.
  4. Right-Click any highlighted song and press add to playlist, then 'New Playlist'.
  5. Click on the Newly created playlist and then click on its name.
  6. Rename it 'Likes' and Save.
  7. Then click Share and copy the link. Paste It Below!
